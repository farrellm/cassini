\chapter{Notation and Conventions}

The notation is that of the first volume~\cite{bronstein2005}, which is cited
throughout by section or by numbered result. Appendix~\ref{app:concordance}
sets it against the notations of the main sources.

\begin{center}

\end{center}

\paragraph{Results.} Theorems, lemmas, definitions and examples are numbered
separately within each section, as in the first volume. Each theorem, lemma,
proposition and corollary has a standing, recorded in
Appendix~\ref{app:standing}: proved here, proved in a cited place, or stated.

\paragraph{Algorithms.} An algorithm is given by its name and arguments, a
specification of its input and output, and its body; comments are written
\textup{(* \dots\ *)}. Algorithms are referred to by name, and the index lists
them.

\paragraph{Later material.} A dagger\later\ on a heading marks material later
than 2005.
