\chapter{Notation and Conventions}

The notation is that of the first volume~\cite{bronstein2005}, which is cited
throughout by section or by numbered result. This page lists what is carried
over; it grows as the chapters are written.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{.68\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
$(k, D)$ & a differential field of characteristic $0$ with derivation $D$\\
$\Const_D(k)$ & the constant subfield of $k$ with respect to $D$\\
$k(t)$ & a monomial extension of $k$: $t$ transcendental over $k$ and $Dt \in k[t]$\\
$\ord{P}$ & the order function at an irreducible $P$\\
$\lc(p)$, $\deg(p)$ & the leading coefficient and the degree of a polynomial $p$\\
$\kbar$ & the algebraic closure of $k$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Results.} Theorems, lemmas, definitions and examples are numbered
separately within each section, as in the first volume.

\paragraph{Algorithms.} An algorithm is given by its name and arguments, a
specification of its input and output, and its body; comments are written
\textup{(* \dots\ *)}. Algorithms are referred to by name.

\paragraph{Later material.} A dagger\later\ on a heading marks material later
than 2005.
