\chapter{From Chapter to Code}\label{app:code}

Bronstein implemented the integration of algebraic functions in the system
that became Axiom. Of the constructors below, all but two carry his name as
author in the Axiom sources, with dates of creation in 1987 and 1988, except
\texttt{HELLFDIV}, dated 1993. The two are the integral-basis packages:
\texttt{FFINTBAS} is by Victor Miller, dated 1990, and \texttt{WFFINTBS} by
Miller and Clifton Williamson, dated 1991. The table
sets each against the part of this book that describes what it computes.
The middle column is the constructor's own one-line description. The right
column is our reading of the correspondence, from those descriptions and
the constructors' names; apart from the Hermite reduction, whose source we
have read (Remark~\ref{rem:trager-form}), we have not checked it against
the code.

The division into \texttt{RADFF} and \texttt{ALGFF}, and into
\texttt{HELLFDIV} and \texttt{FDIV}, is the division between simple radical
extensions and the general case that runs through
Chapters~\ref{ch:divisors} and~\ref{ch:radical}, made into types.
Chapter~\ref{ch:implementations} says how much of this works in the system's
present descendant.
