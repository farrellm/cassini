Three collisions are worth a warning. In Trager's thesis $D$ is a
denominator, a discriminant and a divisor in different chapters, and the
derivation is a prime. In Bronstein's paper of 1990, $K$ is the function
field in Part~1 and $k$ the ground field, whereas in Volume~I and the
tutorial $K$ is the field of constants; and ``normal'' is said there of an
integrand, where this book says ``simple''; Volume~I's ``simple'' is
narrower, and excludes a special factor in the denominator. In Risch's report
$t$ is a ramification index, not a monomial.

The Hermite reduction is indexed differently in every source: with the
denominator $U V^{k+1}$ and $k > 0$ in Trager, $U V^{m+1}$ and $m > 0$ in
Bronstein's paper and report, and $U V^m$ with $m \ge 2$ in the tutorial,
which this book follows.
