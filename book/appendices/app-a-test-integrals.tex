\chapter{Test Integrals}\label{app:test-integrals}

These are the integrals worked in the book, with the verdict reached and the
place where it is reached. Every closed form in the table was checked by
differentiation in a computer algebra system before printing. A verdict of
``not elementary'' cannot be checked that way; it rests on the argument at
the place cited.

The same list is in the file \texttt{data/test-integrals.tsv} of the book's
sources, with integrands and integrals in Wolfram Language syntax, for use
as a test set.

Two further examples are reductions and not integrations. On the curve $y^4 +
(x^2 + x)\,y - x^2 = 0$, the integrand $y^3/x^2$ is brought to a squarefree
denominator by a change of basis alone (Example~\ref{ex:fail-extend}), and
$y^3/x^3$ is reduced by the lazy Hermite reduction to $(2xy^2 - y^3)/x^2$
plus the integral of a function with squarefree denominator
(Example~\ref{ex:lazy-full}). Neither is carried further.
